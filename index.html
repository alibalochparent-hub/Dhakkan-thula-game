🫏 Bhabhi Thulla

Dosto ke saath online card game 🔥
📖 Rules (Roman Urdu)
• 2 se 6 players, 52 patte baant diye jate hain.
• Ace of Spades ♠ wala pehle ye hi patta phenkta hai.
• Baqi sab ko wohi suit phenkni hai. Agar suit nahi hai to koi bhi patta phenko = THULLA 😂
• Sab ne suit follow ki: patte khatam, sab se bada patta wala agla round shuru karta hai.
• Thulla hua: sab se bade lead-suit patte wala saare patte utha leta hai 🫏
• Jis ke patte khatam wo safe. Aakhir me jis ke patte bachen wo BHABHI!
• Patta khelne ke 2 tareeqe: patte ko UPAR swipe karo, ya double tap karo. Single tap sirf utha ta hai.
• Har baari 30 second, warna bot chal deta hai 🤖
